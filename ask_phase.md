# ASK Phase — Cyclistic Case Study

## Business Task
The objective of this analysis is to examine how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently. By identifying behavioral patterns and usage trends between these two customer segments, the analysis aims to generate data-driven insights that will support the development of targeted marketing strategies. These strategies are intended to convert casual riders into annual members, thereby increasing long-term profitability.

## Stakeholders
- Lily Moreno (Director of Marketing): Responsible for campaign strategy and decision-making.
- Cyclistic Executive Team: Evaluates and approves strategic recommendations.
- Marketing Analytics Team: Conducts data analysis and supports marketing initiatives.
