# Final Recommendations — Cyclistic Case Study

## Objective
Convert casual riders into annual members by leveraging behavioral insights derived from trip data.

---

## Recommendation 1: Target Weekend Casual Riders with Membership Promotions

### Insight:
Casual riders predominantly use bikes on weekends and exhibit longer ride durations, indicating leisure-based usage.

### Action:
- Launch targeted weekend promotions offering discounted annual memberships
- Provide limited-time offers immediately after weekend rides via app notifications or email

### Impact:
This targets high-engagement users who already derive significant value from the service, increasing conversion probability.

---

## Recommendation 2: Introduce Cost-Benefit Awareness Campaigns

### Insight:
Casual riders take longer rides, which may result in higher cumulative costs compared to membership plans.

### Action:
- Show users a comparison: "You spent £X this month — membership would cost less"
- Integrate this messaging into the app and email campaigns

### Impact:
Makes the financial benefit explicit, reducing decision friction and encouraging upgrades.

---

## Recommendation 3: Promote Membership for Routine Use

### Insight:
Annual members demonstrate consistent weekday usage, suggesting commuting behavior.

### Action:
- Encourage casual riders to adopt weekday riding through:
  - Free weekday ride trials
  - Commuter-focused campaigns
- Highlight convenience and reliability benefits

### Impact:
Shifts user behavior from occasional to habitual usage, increasing long-term retention and revenue.

---

## Final Conclusion

The analysis reveals clear behavioral differences between casual riders and members in terms of ride duration, frequency, and timing. By targeting high-value casual users with personalized marketing strategies, Cyclistic can effectively increase membership conversion and drive sustainable growth.
