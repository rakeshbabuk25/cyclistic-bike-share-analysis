# Analysis Summary

## Key Findings

1. Casual riders have a higher average ride duration compared to annual members.
2. Annual members take more frequent rides overall.
3. Casual riders are more active on weekends, indicating leisure usage.
4. Members primarily ride on weekdays, suggesting commuting behavior.

## Interpretation

- Casual riders use bikes for recreational purposes.
- Members use bikes as part of their daily routine.

## Business Insight

These behavioral differences suggest that casual riders could be converted into members by targeting frequent or long-duration users with incentives.
