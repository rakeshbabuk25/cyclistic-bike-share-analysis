# Visualization Insights

## 1. Average Ride Length
Casual riders exhibit longer ride durations, indicating leisure-oriented usage.

## 2. Ride Count
Annual members account for a higher number of rides, suggesting habitual usage.

## 3. Rides by Day of Week
Casual riders peak during weekends, while members show consistent weekday usage.

## 4. Average Ride Length by Day
Casual riders maintain higher ride durations across all days, especially weekends.

## Conclusion
Behavioral differences clearly indicate that casual riders use the service for leisure, while members use it for routine commuting.
